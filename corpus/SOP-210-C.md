# SOP-210 Back of House - Opening, Closing, Food Safety, and Cleaning

Ember & Oak Coffee is a fictional company. This is a sample document for software testing only.

## 1 Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This SOP covers back-of-house opening and closing tasks, personal hygiene, food safety, and equipment cleaning. It applies to every team member.

## 2 Personal Hygiene

### 2.1 Handwashing
<!-- step -->
Wash hands for 20 seconds with soap and warm water of at least 100°F (38°C). Dry with a single-use paper towel.

### 2.2 When to wash
Wash at the start of every shift, after using the restroom, after handling cash, after touching your face, hair, or phone, after handling trash, and between allergy orders. Gloves do not replace handwashing.

### 2.3 Appearance
Tie back long hair or wear a cap. Wear a clean apron. Cover cuts with a blue bandage and a glove.

## 3 Opening Procedure (Back of House)

### 3.1 Arrive and clock in
<!-- step -->
Arrive by 5:15 AM for the 6:00 AM opening. Clock in at the back-office terminal.

### 3.2 Check cold storage temperatures
<!-- step -->
Read the walk-in cooler and milk fridge thermometers. Both must read 41°F (5°C) or below. Record each reading on the temperature log. If a reading is above 41°F, tell the shift lead before using any dairy.

### 3.3 Power on the espresso machine
<!-- step -->
Turn on the espresso machine at 5:15 AM. It needs 30 minutes to warm up, so do not pull shots before 5:45 AM.

### 3.4 Set up the grinder
<!-- step -->
Check the bean hopper, purge the first few grams, and set the grind from the dial-in sheet (see SOP-220).

### 3.5 Brew drip coffee
<!-- step -->
Start the first batch of drip coffee in the batch brewer. Label the carafe with the brew time.

### 3.6 Fill the ice bin
<!-- step -->
Fill the ice bin. Keep the ice scoop in its clean holder outside the bin. Never leave the scoop buried in the ice, and never use a cup to scoop ice.

### 3.7 Set up the sanitizer bucket
<!-- step -->
Fill a bucket with quat sanitizer at 200 to 400 ppm. Check the concentration with a test strip. Replace the solution every 4 hours.

### 3.8 Stock the stations
<!-- step -->
Restock cups, lids, sleeves, syrups, and sauces. Follow first in, first out: put new stock behind old stock.

## 4 Food Safety and Labeling

### 4.1 Cold holding
Keep milk and all dairy at 41°F (5°C) or below. If dairy has been above 41°F for more than 4 hours, discard it.

### 4.2 Shelf life after opening
| Item | Use within | Storage |
|---|---|---|
| Dairy milk (whole, 2 percent) | 7 days | 41°F or below |
| Oat, almond, and soy milk | 7 days | 41°F or below |
| Half-and-half | 7 days | 41°F or below |
| Cold brew concentrate | 14 days | 41°F or below |
| Whipped cream canister | 14 days | 41°F or below |
| Mocha sauce | 60 days | Room temperature |
| Flavor syrups | 90 days | Room temperature |
| Matcha powder (opened) | 30 days | Sealed, cool and dry |

Label every opened item with the open date and the discard date.

### 4.3 Brewed drinks
Discard drip coffee 60 minutes after brewing. Never top off an old carafe with fresh coffee.

### 4.4 Ready-to-eat food
Never touch pastries with bare hands. Use tongs or clean gloves.

### 4.5 Spills
Wipe up spills right away and set out the yellow wet-floor sign.

## 5 Cleaning the Espresso Machine (Daily)

### 5.1 Backflush the group heads
<!-- step -->
Insert the blind basket, add 3 g (about half a teaspoon) of espresso machine cleaner, and run the pump 10 seconds on and 10 seconds off, five times. Then repeat five times with plain water only.

### 5.2 Soak the portafilters
<!-- step -->
Soak portafilters and baskets in cleaner solution for 20 minutes, then rinse well.

### 5.3 Clean the steam wand
<!-- step -->
After every use, wipe the steam wand with a sanitized cloth and purge it for 2 seconds. At close, also remove the steam tip and soak it in cleaner solution for 20 minutes.

### 5.4 Knock box and drip tray
<!-- step -->
Empty and wash the knock box and the drip tray.

### 5.5 Grinder
Brush out the burr chamber every day. Do a full grinder deep clean every Sunday at close.

## 6 Closing Procedure (Back of House)

### 6.1 Last call
Last call is 7:45 PM. The café closes at 8:00 PM.

### 6.2 Pull and discard
<!-- step -->
Discard all brewed drip coffee. Pour out leftover steamed milk. Return opened, labeled milk to the fridge.

### 6.3 Three-compartment sink
<!-- step -->
Wash in the first sink with soapy water at 110°F (43°C). Rinse in the second sink with clear water. Sanitize in the third sink with quat sanitizer at 200 to 400 ppm for 60 seconds. Air dry. Do not towel dry.

### 6.4 Floors and trash
<!-- step -->
Empty the trash and recycling, sweep, and mop with sanitizer solution.

### 6.5 Lock up
<!-- step -->
Lock the back door, turn off the espresso machine and the lights, and set the alarm. The manager gives out the alarm code.

## 7 Injuries and Emergencies

### 7.1 Burns
Cool a burn under cool running water for at least 10 minutes. Tell the shift lead. Report every injury to the manager the same day.

### 7.2 First aid kit and fire extinguisher
The first aid kit is under the back counter by the dish sink. The fire extinguisher is on the wall by the back door.

### 7.3 Fire
Call 911, leave the building, and meet at the parking lot corner.
