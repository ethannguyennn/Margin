# SOP-201 Cashier - Station Setup and Order Taking

Ember & Oak Coffee is a fictional company. This is a sample document for software testing only.

## 1 Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This SOP tells new cashiers how to set up the register, greet guests, take accurate orders, and handle allergy requests. It applies to every cashier at Ember & Oak Coffee.

### 1.2 Related documents
Payments, refunds, and closing the till are in SOP-202. Espresso and milk standards are in SOP-220. Drink recipes are in SOP-230.

## 2 Register Setup Before Opening

### 2.1 Log in to the POS
<!-- step -->
Enter your personal 4-digit employee PIN on the POS. Never share your PIN or use someone else's.

### 2.2 Verify the drawer float
<!-- step -->
Count the cash drawer. The opening float is $150.00: two $20 bills, three $10 bills, six $5 bills, thirty $1 bills, and two rolls of quarters ($10.00 each). If the float is off by any amount, tell the shift lead before opening.

### 2.3 Check the card reader and printer
<!-- step -->
Confirm the card reader is charged above 50 percent and the receipt printer has a full roll of paper. Spare paper rolls are in the cabinet under the register.

### 2.4 Review the 86 list
<!-- step -->
Ask the shift lead which items are out of stock (86'd). Mark them unavailable in the POS so they cannot be rung in.

### 2.5 Wash your hands
<!-- step -->
Wash your hands for 20 seconds with soap and warm water before touching the register or any food item.

## 3 Greeting and Taking Orders

### 3.1 Greet the guest
<!-- step -->
Greet every guest within 10 seconds of their reaching the counter. Make eye contact, smile, and say "Welcome to Ember & Oak."

### 3.2 Take the name
<!-- step -->
Ask for the guest's first name on every drink order and write it on the cup or type it in the POS name field.

### 3.3 Ring the order in sequence
<!-- step -->
Enter items in this order: size, drink, milk, then modifiers. Example: a large oat vanilla latte is entered as Large, Latte, Oat, Vanilla.

### 3.4 Repeat the order back
<!-- step -->
Read the full order back to the guest and confirm the total before taking payment.

### 3.5 Offer one suggestion
<!-- step -->
Offer a single add-on, such as a pastry. Never offer more than one suggestion per guest, and do not repeat it if the guest says no.

## 4 Sizes, Prices, and Modifiers

### 4.1 Cup sizes
Small is 12 oz, Medium is 16 oz, and Large is 20 oz. Flat White is sold in Small and Medium only.

### 4.3 Modifier charges
Oat, almond, or soy milk: add $0.75. Extra espresso shot: add $1.00. Flavor syrup (vanilla, caramel, or hazelnut): add $0.60. Whipped cream, decaf, and extra hot are free.

### 4.4 Sales tax
The POS adds 9.5 percent sales tax automatically. Never enter tax by hand.

## 5 Allergies and Special Requests

### 5.1 Ask and flag
If a guest mentions an allergy, press the ALLERGY button in the POS before sending the order. The barista will change gloves and use a clean pitcher and a freshly sanitized steam wand.

### 5.2 What you can say
Never promise that a drink is allergen-free. Say: "We prepare drinks in a shared kitchen that handles milk, soy, tree nuts, wheat, and eggs."

### 5.3 Allergen binder
The allergen binder is kept in the drawer under the register. Show it to the guest on request.

## 6 Wait Times and Mobile Orders

### 6.1 Target wait time
Drinks should be ready within 4 minutes of payment.

### 6.2 Long waits
If an order has waited more than 6 minutes, apologize to the guest and tell the shift lead. The shift lead may comp a drip coffee.

### 6.3 Mobile orders
Mobile orders appear with an orange banner on the POS. Accept each one within 60 seconds. Hand off mobile orders by order number, not by name.
