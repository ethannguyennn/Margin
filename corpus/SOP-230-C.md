# SOP-230 Drink Recipes

Ember & Oak Coffee is a fictional company. This is a sample document for software testing only.

## 1 General Rules

### 1.1 Sizes
Small is 12 oz, Medium is 16 oz, and Large is 20 oz. Flat White is made in Small and Medium only.

### 1.2 Pump sizes
One pump of flavor syrup is 1/4 fl oz (7.5 mL). One pump of mocha sauce is 1/2 fl oz (15 mL).

### 1.3 Flavor syrup amounts
Flavored drinks (vanilla, caramel, or hazelnut) get 2 pumps in Small, 3 pumps in Medium, and 4 pumps in Large.

### 1.4 Ice line
Every iced drink is filled with ice up to the ice line marked on the cold cup.

## 2 Latte (Hot)

### 2.1 Latte specifications
| | Small | Medium | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| Espresso shots | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Steamed milk | 8 oz | 12 oz | 14 oz |

Foam is about 1/4 inch of microfoam. If a flavor is ordered, add syrup using the pump amounts in section 1.3.

### 2.2 Latte: add syrup
<!-- step -->
If flavored, pump the syrup into the empty cup.

### 2.3 Latte: pull espresso
<!-- step -->
Pull the shots (see SOP-220) directly into the cup. Stir if there is syrup.

### 2.4 Latte: steam milk
<!-- step -->
Steam the milk to its target temperature (see SOP-220), stretching for 2 to 4 seconds.

### 2.5 Latte: pour
<!-- step -->
Pour the milk into the espresso, finishing with a small heart or dot of foam on top. Add the lid and call the name.

## 3 Cappuccino

### 3.1 Cappuccino specifications
| | Small | Medium | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| Espresso shots | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Steamed milk | 5 oz | 7 oz | 9 oz |

Foam is about 3/4 inch (2 cm).

### 3.2 Cappuccino: pull espresso
<!-- step -->
Pull the shots (see SOP-220) directly into the cup.

### 3.3 Cappuccino: steam milk
<!-- step -->
Steam the milk to its target temperature, stretching for 6 to 8 seconds to build thick foam.

### 3.4 Cappuccino: pour
<!-- step -->
Pour the milk into the espresso and spoon on extra foam until the foam is about 3/4 inch deep. Add the lid.

## 4 Flat White

### 4.1 Flat white specifications
| | Small | Medium | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| Espresso shots | 2 | 3 | not made |
| Steamed milk | 6 oz | 8 oz | not made |

Foam is about 1/8 inch of thin microfoam.

### 4.2 Flat white: pull espresso
<!-- step -->
Pull the shots (see SOP-220) directly into the cup.

### 4.3 Flat white: steam milk
<!-- step -->
Steam the milk to its target temperature, stretching for only 1 to 2 seconds.

### 4.4 Flat white: pour
<!-- step -->
Pour the milk in one smooth stream so the microfoam blends with the espresso. Add the lid.

## 5 Americano

### 5.1 Americano specifications
| | Small | Medium | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| Espresso shots | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Hot water | 8 oz | 12 oz | 14 oz |

Hot water comes from the tap set to 195°F (90°C).

### 5.2 Americano: add water
<!-- step -->
Fill the cup with the hot water first.

### 5.3 Americano: add espresso
<!-- step -->
Pull the shots (see SOP-220) and pour them gently on top so the crema stays on the surface.

## 6 Iced Latte

### 6.1 Iced latte specifications
| | Small | Medium | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| Espresso shots | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Cold milk | 6 oz | 8 oz | 10 oz |

### 6.2 Iced latte: add syrup and milk
<!-- step -->
Pump syrup into the cold cup if flavored, then pour in the cold milk.

### 6.3 Iced latte: add ice
<!-- step -->
Fill the cup with ice up to the ice line.

### 6.4 Iced latte: add espresso
<!-- step -->
Pull the shots (see SOP-220), pour them over the ice, and stir. Add the lid.

## 7 Mocha

### 7.1 Mocha specifications
| | Small | Medium | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| Espresso shots | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Mocha sauce | 2 pumps | 3 pumps | 4 pumps |
| Steamed milk | 8 oz | 10 oz | 12 oz |

Whipped cream is optional and free.

### 7.2 Mocha: add sauce
<!-- step -->
Pump the mocha sauce into the empty cup.

### 7.3 Mocha: pull espresso
<!-- step -->
Pull the shots (see SOP-220) into the cup and stir until the sauce is dissolved.

### 7.4 Mocha: steam and pour
<!-- step -->
Steam the milk to its target temperature, pour it in, and top with whipped cream if the guest wants it.

## 8 Cold Brew

### 8.1 Cold brew specifications
| | Small | Medium | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold brew concentrate | 4 oz | 6 oz | 8 oz |
| Water | 4 oz | 6 oz | 8 oz |

Pour over ice up to the ice line. The mix is 1 part concentrate to 1 part water.

### 8.2 Cold brew: serve
<!-- step -->
Fill the cup with ice up to the ice line, pour in the concentrate and then the water, and stir once.

### 8.3 Cold brew batch recipe
<!-- step -->
Combine 1 lb (454 g) of coarse ground coffee with 1 gallon of cold water. Steep for 18 hours in the walk-in at 41°F or below. Filter, label with the date, and use within 14 days.

## 9 Matcha Latte

### 9.1 Matcha specifications
| | Small | Medium | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matcha powder | 3 g | 4 g | 5 g |
| Hot water for the slurry | 2 oz | 2 oz | 2 oz |
| Steamed milk (hot) | 8 oz | 12 oz | 14 oz |
| Cold milk (iced) | 6 oz | 8 oz | 10 oz |

The hot water for the slurry is 175°F (80°C). Matcha is not sweetened unless the guest asks for syrup.

### 9.2 Matcha: sift
<!-- step -->
Sift the matcha powder into the cup to remove lumps.

### 9.3 Matcha: whisk
<!-- step -->
Add 2 oz of 175°F water and whisk in a W motion until the slurry is smooth with no lumps.

### 9.4 Matcha: add milk
<!-- step -->
For a hot drink, pour in steamed milk. For an iced drink, add cold milk and ice up to the ice line. Add the lid.

## 10 Drip Coffee

### 10.1 Drip coffee service
<!-- step -->
Fill the cup from the carafe, leaving 1/2 inch of space below the rim for cream. Add a sleeve to hot cups. Cream and sugar are at the condiment bar. Discard drip coffee 60 minutes after brewing (see SOP-210).
