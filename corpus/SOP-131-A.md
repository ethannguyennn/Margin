# Halcyon HX-2 portable fluid pump: Torque Wrench Calibration Check

## 1 Scope
This fictional training SOP checks the click wrench used for HX-2 mounting bracket assembly before each training shift. It is a verification check, not an adjustment or calibration service. All names, parts, limits, and records are synthetic demo data.

## 2 Equipment
| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Click wrench | 1 N-m to 10 N-m operating range |
| Torque analyzer | Current calibration label; resolution 0.01 N-m |
| Adapter | 4 mm hex; no extension bar |

## 3 Warning
> WARNING: Never adjust the wrench during this check. A dropped wrench or failed check requires a red HOLD tag and removal from service. Use SOP-140 to record the problem. Do not use a failed wrench on product.

## 4 Procedure
### 4.1 Inspect labels and condition
1. Confirm the wrench and torque analyzer calibration labels have not expired. Inspect the handle, ratchet, and 4 mm adapter for damage. Record both asset numbers on tool-check form TC-131 before loading the wrench.

### 4.2 Prepare the analyzer
2. Secure the torque analyzer to bench T1. Zero the unloaded display. Set the click wrench to 4 N-m and fit the 4 mm adapter without an extension bar. Apply load only in the clockwise direction.

### 4.3 Exercise the wrench
3. Apply 3 smooth clockwise pulls to the first click, releasing all load after each pull. These are conditioning pulls; do not include them in the recorded readings.

### 4.4 Check three readings
4. Record the next 3 clockwise first-click readings. Each reading must be from 3.84 N-m to 4.16 N-m inclusive. Do not average the readings to hide a failure. If any reading is outside the range, tag the wrench HOLD and report it under SOP-140.

### 4.5 Release or quarantine
5. If all 3 readings pass, sign TC-131 and mark the wrench CHECKED for the current training shift. If any check fails, put the wrench in the locked tool quarantine cabinet and record a deviation under SOP-140.

## 5 Storage
After use, return the wrench setting to 1 N-m and place it in its case. This document does not specify calibration intervals, repair instructions, or counterclockwise acceptance limits.
