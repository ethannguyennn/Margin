# Halcyon HX-2 portable fluid pump: Mounting Bracket Assembly

## 1 Scope
This fictional training SOP installs bracket HX2-210 on an accepted HX-2 pump body. Revision D is a draft for an isolated fork demonstration only. All names, parts, limits, and records are synthetic demo data. No other fastener size or pump model is covered.

## 2 Tools and parts
| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Fasteners | 4 M5 x 12 screws, HX2-212 |
| Washers | 4 flat washers, HX2-213 |
| Driver | 4 mm hex bit |
| Torque wrench | 1 N-m to 10 N-m range; check per SOP-131 |

## 3 Warning
> WARNING: Isolate power before assembly. Do not lubricate threads or substitute fasteners. Stop if a screw binds; forcing it can damage the pump housing. Log damage under SOP-140.

## 4 Procedure
### 4.1 Prepare and seat the bracket
1. Disconnect power. Confirm the green RECEIVED label from SOP-101. Check the torque wrench under SOP-131. Seat bracket HX2-210 with the arrow toward the outlet. Fit all four washers and start all four M5 screws by hand for 3 full turns. Keep threads dry.

### 4.2 Fasten the M5 bracket screws
2. Torque all four M5 bracket screws to 5 N-m in a star pattern using a calibrated torque wrench (see SOP-131). Tighten in the order upper left, lower right, upper right, lower left. Stop at the first click on each screw; do not click twice.

### 4.3 Check the bracket gap
3. Check the gap between the bracket foot and housing with a 0.20 mm feeler gauge. The gauge must not enter at any of the four screw positions. If it enters, stop and report the gap under SOP-140; do not add torque.

### 4.4 Apply witness marks
4. Apply one blue witness line across each screw head and onto the bracket. Each line must be continuous and visible from the front. Do not mark the pump serial label.

### 4.5 Check outlet clearance
5. Measure the clearance between the bracket edge and outlet collar. Accept at least 6 mm clearance. Route the disconnected power lead outside the bracket opening without pinching it.

### 4.6 Record assembly
6. Record the pump serial number, wrench asset number, 5 N-m setting, and assembler initials on assembly form AS-114. Record revision D. Do not record a measured torque when only a wrench setting was used.

### 4.7 Release to the next station
7. Confirm all four witness marks and the completed AS-114. Place the pump in the ASSEMBLED tray. Keep power disconnected; this procedure does not authorize a powered run test.

### 4.8 Obtain independent signoff
8. Before moving the ASSEMBLED tray, have a second inspector check all four witness marks and sign the independent-check box on AS-114. Keep the pump on hold until that signature is present.

## 5 Related procedures
SOP-101 controls receiving acceptance. SOP-131 controls the wrench check. SOP-140 controls nonconformance reporting. This procedure contains no vibration test or powered performance test limits.
