# Halcyon HX-2 portable fluid pump: Cleaning and Disinfection

## 1 Scope
This fictional training SOP covers external HX-2 housing surfaces at the end of a training shift. It does not specify internal fluid-path cleaning, sterilization, or clinical use. All names, parts, limits, and records are synthetic demo data.

## 2 Supplies
| Supply | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Cleaning cloth | Nonabrasive, lint-free |
| Water | Filtered through a 5 µm filter |
| Disinfectant | Premixed 70 % isopropyl alcohol wipe |
| Timer | Resolution of 1 s |

## 3 Warning
> WARNING: Disconnect power. Never immerse the pump or spray liquid into the outlet or power connector. Keep alcohol away from ignition sources. This process is disinfection of external training surfaces, not sterilization.

## 4 Procedure
### 4.1 Isolate and cool
1. Disconnect power and remove the pump from the training fixture. Allow the housing to cool below 35 °C before wiping. Keep the outlet cap installed throughout cleaning.

### 4.2 Remove visible soil
2. Dampen a lint-free cloth with water filtered through a 5 µm filter. Wipe visible soil from the external housing until no residue remains. Do not allow free liquid to run into seams or connectors.

### 4.3 Disinfect the housing
3. Wipe the external housing with a premixed 70 % isopropyl alcohol wipe. Keep the surface visibly wet for 60 s. Use a fresh wipe if the surface dries before the contact time ends. Do not dilute the premixed wipe.

### 4.4 Air dry
4. Allow the housing to air dry for at least 5 min after the contact time. Do not use compressed air, heat, or a dry cloth to shorten drying. Leave power disconnected during drying.

### 4.5 Inspect and record
5. Inspect the dry housing for cracks, sticky residue, and damaged labels. Record the pump serial number, wipe lot, date, and operator initials on cleaning form CL-120. Report any damage under SOP-140 before reuse.

## 5 Records
Retain CL-120 for 12 months. Record completion at the end of each training shift; no schedule for other pump models is defined here.
