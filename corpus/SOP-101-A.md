# Halcyon HX-2 portable fluid pump: Receiving Inspection

## 1 Scope
This fictional training SOP covers unopened HX-2 kits at receiving station R1. It does not authorize functional testing, repair, or another pump model. All names, parts, limits, and records are synthetic demo data.

## 2 Kit contents
| Item | Part number | Quantity per kit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HX-2 pump body | HX2-100 | 1 |
| Mounting bracket | HX2-210 | 1 |
| M5 x 12 bracket screw | HX2-212 | 4 |
| Flat washer | HX2-213 | 4 |

## 3 Warning
> WARNING: Do not energize an incoming pump. Quarantine a wet carton, broken tamper seal, or unreadable serial label. Do not substitute a missing part from another kit.

## 4 Procedure
### 4.1 Verify shipment identity
1. Compare the carton model with HX-2 and match the serial number to the packing list. Record the purchase order and serial number on receiving form RI-101 before opening the carton.

### 4.2 Inspect carton and seal
2. Inspect all six carton faces. Quarantine the kit if a dent is deeper than 10 mm, the carton is wet, or the tamper seal is broken. Record the defect under SOP-140 before moving the kit.

### 4.3 Count kit contents
3. Count 1 pump body, 1 mounting bracket, 4 M5 x 12 screws, and 4 flat washers against section 2. Keep the parts in the labeled tray. Quarantine any kit with a missing or mixed part.

### 4.4 Inspect bracket bore
4. Measure the bracket locating bore with a calibrated bore gauge. Accept a diameter from 8.00 mm to 8.10 mm inclusive. Record the measured diameter on RI-101; quarantine a value outside this range.

### 4.5 Label accepted kit
5. Apply a green RECEIVED label only after sections 4.1 through 4.4 pass. Record the inspector initials and receiving date. Place the kit on shelf R1-A for assembly under SOP-114.

## 5 Records
Store RI-101 with the shipment record for 12 months. A RECEIVED label confirms incoming inspection only; it does not authorize operating the pump.
